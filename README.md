# Social Media Analytics – Case Studies

## Overview

This repository presents two academic case studies exploring real-world issues in social media, focusing on crisis communication and the spread of misinformation.

## Case Studies

### 1. Social Media Analytics for Crisis Communication: MV Wakashio Oil Spill

Explores the role of social media in crisis communication during the MV Wakashio oil spill, examining information sharing, public engagement and communication challenges.

### 2. The Spread of Supplement and "Miracle Weight Loss" Misinformation on Social Media

Examines how misleading supplement and weight-loss claims spread across social media and explores the role of online platforms in influencing public understanding.
